Top two panels: published crown-age intervals from three molecular-clock studies [@morris2018TimescaleEarlyLand; @harris2022DivergentEvolutionaryTrajectories; @hooper2026ReconstructingSporeEvolution], with the three calibration maxima examined here (dashed). The bars do not share one statistical meaning: Morris et al.'s are envelopes of 95% highest-posterior-density intervals across seven topologies; the others are the intervals each study reports. Bottom: first appearances by source and reading. Pale bars run from the oldest possible to the oldest certain occurrence. Body bars are the reviewed locality units of this study: land-plant bodies including the Katian Oman sporangial fragments (canonical) [@wellman2003FragmentsEarliestLand] and, for comparison, excluding them. Dark bars mark the span after the oldest certain occurrence, not a continuous record. The 459.1 Ma maximum is the GTS2012 Sandbian base plus its uncertainty. The hatched bar marks Cambrian Stage 4 cryptospore-like forms, whose land-plant affinity none of the sources examined accepts.](../figures/fig1_problem.png){#fig-problem width=100%}
