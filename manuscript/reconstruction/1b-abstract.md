Ordovician. We examined three fossil maximum ages: 515.5, 459.1 and 431.2 Ma. We combined source audits, a Libyan

[8]{.C48 .V054}–[12]{.C48 .V055} times lower, or [2.4]{.C48 .V056}–[4.5]{.C48 .V057} under alternative
