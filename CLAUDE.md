## Delegation and card review

The independent Codex desktop session is a direct scientific collaborator, including synthesis,
criticism and judgement, under Barry's 2026-10-28 instruction (see `AGENTS.md`, “Direct collaboration”).

Barry confirmed on 2026-10-29 that the Board cadence is asynchronous: post results and an
owned-file handoff, then Barry re-prompts the other collaborator. A Board post does not dispatch
work. Finish the owned tranche and report material findings in the chat; do not wait or poll for
an unprompted collaborator.

Keep source-critical synthesis (cards, reviews, the primer), judgement and integration in the
main thread by default. Delegate bounded, checkable work when useful, such as structured
extraction, classification, digitisation, crosswalks and table-building. Choose delegates
according to the task and available capabilities.
For Codex jobs, the `codex-delegate` skill and `~/.claude/bin/codex-delegate` document the run
command, prompts, schemas, chunking, verification, cross-lab audit and job limits.

- Have an independent reviewer check card claims against their sources before marking a card
  `reviewed`, as specified in `AGENTS.md`. Use cross-lab review where useful. The drafting
  agent then adjudicates every finding against the source. The 2026-10-28 bake-off
  (`docs/audits/2026-10-28-card-bakeoff.md`) kept drafting on Claude: Sol's draft was accurate
  but thin.
- Record pilots and audits in `docs/audits/` (see `2026-10-27-codex-pilot.md`).
