## In brief

*Eophyllophyton bellum* is a small Pragian plant from Yunnan whose fertile and sterile lateral appendages are flattened, deeply dissected and veined, and were first described as the oldest megaphyll leaves. Reanalysis shows the appendages are reduced three-dimensional branch systems flattened only at their tips, so the plant is better read as a leaf precursor than a leaf. It anchors the early end of the debate on when laminate photosynthetic surfaces arose in euphyllophytes.

## Morphology and anatomy

Observed in compressions: lateral branches bearing small laterals that end in pairs of dissected appendages with branching veins and elongated mesophyll cells lacking palisade and spongy layers (Hao et al. 2003; leaf-pair figure in Hao & Xue 2013). Secondary summaries of that paper give blade dimensions of about 1.7-4.8 mm wide and 2.2-5.0 mm long (not checked against the paper). The 2003 interpretation was that divided blades arranged in several planes show leaves evolving from an axial branching system. Fertile appendages carry spherical sporangia in rows.

Interpretation has shifted. Hao & Xue (2013) recognise at least five euphyllophyte lineages in the Pragian of South China, each with different leaf precursors ("branch-leaf complexes"), and infer multiple origins of megaphylls. Tomescu & Whitewoods (2024), using the same anatomy, score the appendages for only one leaf-defining character, determinate growth: no regular arrangement, radially symmetrical vascularisation, three-dimensional architecture, flattening restricted to ultimate segments, no dorsiventral polarity. Hao (1988) named the genus; compilations citing 'Hao & Beck 1993' for the species are citing the later 'further observations' paper, so species authorship should be checked against the nomenclatural record.

## Age and distribution

Known only from the Posongchong Formation, Wenshan, Yunnan, in a flora of 28 genera (Cascales-Miñana et al. 2017). Palynology, fish and overlying marine fauna place it in the middle to late Pragian; no radiometric age. Co-occurring early euphyllophytes include *Psilophyton primitivum*, *Pauthecophyton gracile*, *Hedeia sinica* and the sphenopsid-lineage *Estinnophyton yunnanense*. *Pauthecophyton* also occurs in Guizhou (Wang et al. 2022); I found no *Eophyllophyton* outside Yunnan. Pragian-Emsian warmth coincided with more cosmopolitan floras globally (Capel et al. 2023), yet the South China flora has many endemic members (Wang et al. 2022).

## Affinity and controversies

A basal euphyllophyte, but its closest relatives are unresolved; Hao & Xue (2013) reportedly erected an isolated class for it. The contest is not placement but leaf status. Hao et al. (2003) argued the small size and deeply incised margins were adaptations to heat stress at high CO2; Tomescu & Whitewoods (2024) conclude that true euphyllophyte leaves appear about 25 Myr after lycophyte leaves (about 410 Ma), towards the end of the Middle Devonian, and that Early Devonian lateral systems show a mosaic of leaf characters.

## Ecology and physiology

Little is known: stomata unobserved (no epidermis in SEM), no roots, spores or gametophyte reported in the sources read. The mesophyll's uniform, undifferentiated structure suggests modest photosynthetic specialisation (an inference).

## Why it matters for modelling

- **First laminate surface.** If the appendages count, laminate photosynthetic surfaces in euphyllophytes date to about 410 Ma; if not, to the Givetian, about 385 Ma. A model should run both, since the 25 Myr difference is large compared with the Early Devonian stage durations in data/timescale.csv (Pragian 2.4 Myr, Emsian 17 Myr).
- **Size scale.** Blades of a few millimetres (bounding area at most about 24 mm2 from the quoted dimensions, less after dissection) are the unit; a plant-level photosynthetic area needs counts not yet found.
- **CO2 premise.** The heat-stress explanation relies on high CO2 limiting stomatal conductance; Osborne et al. (2004) report a 25-fold blade enlargement and an 8-fold stomatal density rise as CO2 fell. Dahl et al. (2022) estimate 525-715 ppm for 410-380 Ma (±100 ppm), against the canonical view of about an order of magnitude above pre-industrial, and do not discuss leaf origin. The delay in megaphylls then needs developmental or hydraulic explanations (Boyce & Knoll 2002; Galtier 2010); this is my inference, not a published conclusion.
- **Parallel origins.** Several leaf-precursor lineages appear simultaneously in the Pragian of one region, so the trait may have been easily gained and lost.

## Model transfer

**Chosen to contrast:** Contrast a vascularised branch-leaf precursor with a dorsiventral laminate leaf, using appendages flattened only at their tips to test alternative timings and anatomical requirements of leaf acquisition.

- **Can transfer:**
  - For Eophyllophyton bellum compressions from the Posongchong Formation, Wenshan, represent paired dissected appendages with branching veins and mesophyll lacking palisade and spongy layers (Hao et al. 2003; Hao & Xue 2013).
  - For Eophyllophyton bellum from Posongchong, use the character interpretation of determinate growth with radial vascular symmetry, three-dimensional architecture and no dorsiventral polarity, rather than a binary mature-leaf character (Tomescu & Whitewoods 2024).
  - Use middle to late Pragian as the Posongchong occurrence interval for Eophyllophyton bellum, constrained by palynology, fish and overlying marine fauna; this is not a radiometric date (Cascales-Miñana et al. 2017).
- **Cannot transfer:**
  - The front-matter megaphyll state cannot establish a true leaf: this card's later interpretation recognises reduced branch systems and incomplete leaf characters.
  - Quoted blade dimensions and the derived bounding area cannot become verified primary measurements: the dimensions came from unchecked secondary summaries.
  - Stomatal density, conductance and measured heat tolerance cannot be assigned: no epidermis was observed, and the high-CO2 heat-stress explanation is an interpretation.
  - Plant-level photosynthetic area cannot be inferred from one appendage's dimensions without appendage counts.
