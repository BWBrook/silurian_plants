## Required revisions

### C1. Keep the spore/body separation conditional and define the body inventory

C28's subtraction is correct: 458.2 minus the four bounded B0 endpoints gives about 21–24 Myr.
The endpoints depend on a chosen recovery history, unit independence, reported age support and
an operational body inventory. They are confidence bounds on recoverability, not dates of first
biological bodies or an observed lag duration. Calling the result a known-answer check does not
remove those assumptions.

The abstract says bodies “demonstrably lagged”; main §4.1 calls the lag an observed feature;
the conclusion repeats it; brief finding 4 says “We know”; and agent-guide §2 says this shows
a lag happened. Replace each with the conditional comparison. Suggested core wording:

> Under the four bounded recovery histories, the selected body-record endpoints are 21–24 Myr
> younger than the strict cryptospore benchmark. This separation shows what those histories and
> that inventory imply; it does not estimate a biological delay or validate the recovery model.

The selected inventory is not literally every land-plant body remain. `build_reviewed.py`
selects curated events E3–E5, whereas the Oman spore-filled sporangial fragments are in E2
(`data/occurrences/observations.csv`, S-w22-K12). Retain the existing analysis, define its
operational scope in §2/S5.1, and use a precise label in all renderings and figures. A decision
to include fragments requires a separate inventory change and rerun; it cannot be accomplished
by relabelling the existing B0 result. C28 is not ready for promotion in its present wording.

### C2. Limit the 0.09 product statement to H0c

C24 and main §3.2/Table 1 combine H0c and H0. For H0c the product rho*f is approximately 0.091
at each declared delivery ratio. For H0 the listed products are approximately 0.184, 0.129,
0.105 and 0.092. “The product stays near 0.09” is therefore not a statement about both history
classes. The abstract's “a tenth” and §3.2's sustained-producer interpretation must name the
constant history; the exposure-weighted history gives about 0.184 at equal delivery.

Retain the supported encounter-identification result, without implying an intensity bound at
every time in the Late Ordovician. Brief finding 2 also needs the sustained-history and
within-basin scope. Correct C24's value/limit fields before propagating the wording into the
article, brief and agent guide. The independent audit gives the numerical reconstruction.

### C3. QSIM bounds a persistent minimum ratio, not a pooled percentage

Main §3.2, lines 159–164, says about 3% and 31% “of land-plant spores”. Supplement S3 correctly
defines theta as target/control intensity and the estimand as the minimum across samples.
The h=0.1 value 0.3072 is a trilete:cryptospore ratio; its corresponding target fraction is
0.3072/(1+0.3072) = 0.2350, not 0.31. At h=1, 0.03072/(1+0.03072) = 0.02980.

Use “a trilete-to-cryptospore ratio sustained above 0.031 (equal effective recovery), or 0.307
(at one-tenth effective recovery), is incompatible under the declared exploratory model”.
Do not turn a minimum-ratio bound into a bound on every sample, an average or the pooled
assemblage. Keep the unverified co-ascertainment/independence qualification. The stored C12
numerical results need no rerun.

### C4. Integrate the published Borenshult alternatives without conflating identification and nodes

The parent inspected Rubinstein & Vajda (2019), printed pp.183–184 (PDF pp.4–5), as rendered
pages. Figure 3 has six definite trilete depths (43.1, 41.9, 41.1, 40.35, 40.2, 33.05 m) and
two possible-only depths (48.2, 34 m). Figure 4 has no trilete taxon at 41.1 m. Preserve both
literal reports. Graphical depth matching is schematic; the 40.35 m symbol is offset on a
leader line. Symbols and positive sample depths are not specimen counts.

The Figure 3 alternative is a citable reading of published evidence. It does not automatically
replace the older strict compilation or settle crown membership. Figure 1 must distinguish
which reading supports each first-appearance bar, rather than presenting the strict trilete
bar as the sole published account. A separate authors' reading overlay or clearly labelled
alternative is appropriate; do not silently move the frozen main bar.

Main §3.2 should include Hooper's Supplementary Table 4 derivation: 458.4 + 0.7 = 459.1 Ma,
from the historical Sandbian stage base. The same stage-base rule applies to the definite
43.1 m level, so accepting it does not itself change that maximum. The possible crown-minimum
consequence is a separate conditional argument requiring a justified character-to-crown link
and an explicit treatment of the bentonite date's uncertainty. “About 454.4 Ma” is not already
an adopted hard calibration minimum. Main §3.2's “trilete monads” also needs alignment with S6:
aperture and dispersal unit were reconstructed separately, not as a joint ancestral phenotype.

Figure 2 and `mapping.csv` currently put Murzuq/QSIM in M2's “observed material” cell. Include
Borenshult, the material behind the calibration, and distinguish it from the samples used to
audit the absence implication. Otherwise the schematic omits the pivotal specimen link.

### C5. Show the age-treatment sensitivity of recovery-shape adequacy

The independent methods audit identifies selective presentation in §3.3 and Table S7. The
reported constant-recovery nominal p values of 0.073–0.765 are the 5th–95th percentiles under
independent-uniform age allocations. The same frozen file gives midpoint p = 0.00848 and
shared-quantile p values with 5th–95th percentiles 3.1e-9–0.0165. Constant recovery is therefore
not consistently adequate across the declared age treatments. The parent verified these rows
directly in `models/f3/loop3/out/shape_diagnostics.csv`.

Expose all declared age treatments in the main interpretation and supplement. The strong
decline10 misfit does not validate constant recovery or select it as a calibrated model.
Keep nominal exact-age tests, imputation summaries and geological dating assumptions distinct;
none supplies a combined test for interval-dated fossils. The quoted p-value span is not the
minimum-to-maximum range across all draws.

### C6. Keep suppression and delay magnitudes illustrative

C32's tenfold reduction is a post hoc sufficient scenario, not an estimated recovery change
or required threshold. C33's approximately 15–23 Myr differences are minimum assumed delays
needed to reach the named clock ages at the constant-recovery endpoint bound. They are not
fitted delays. State those distinctions in §3.3–4.1 and the brief. The arithmetic can be
accepted after its estimand is made explicit; it does not require rerunning the endpoint code.

third is the rubric's reviewer-proposed concordant subset, with 41.1 m unresolved rather than
