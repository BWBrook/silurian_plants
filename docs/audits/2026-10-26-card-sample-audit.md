# Independent audit of four taxon cards (silurian_plants)

Audited 2026-10-26. Cards: `taxa/cooksonia.md`, `taxa/baragwanathia-longifolia.md`, `taxa/psilophyton.md`, `taxa/ordovician-cryptospores.md`. No repo files were edited.

Method: each claim was checked against the cited source's abstract and, where open, the full text (publisher page, PMC, institutional repositories, supplements), or against another authoritative source. Verdict key: SUPPORTED, MISATTRIBUTED (true but the cited source does not say it), OVERSTATED/IMPRECISE, WRONG, UNVERIFIABLE (no accessible source). All reference DOIs in all four cards (about 85) were resolved through the Crossref API by script; the three-per-card detail is in the last section.

Sources read in full or in substantial part: Hooper et al. 2026 (publisher HTML), Libertin et al. 2018 (abstract, supplement, Fossil Imprint companion), Garza et al. 2024, Edwards & Feehan 1980 (abstract), Edwards et al. 1992 (abstract, refs), Edwards & Kenrick 2015, Kenrick et al. 2012, Pšenička et al. 2021, Tims & Chambers 1984 (OCR/PDF), McSweeney et al. 2022, Earp 2024 (via fetch), Kraft & Kvacek 2021, Bek et al. 2024, Doran 1980 (abstract), Doran & Tomescu 2025, Tomescu & Whitewoods 2024, Wellman et al. 2003, Wellman et al. 2015, Morris et al. 2018, plus abstracts for most others.
Not accessible (paywalled or blocked): Libertin et al. 2018 main text, Rubinstein & Vajda 2019, Strother et al. 2015, Edwards et al. 2014 and 2021 full texts, Cascales-Minana et al. 2017 full text, Jaeger 1966 original, Lang & Cookson 1935, Banks et al. 1975, Taylor et al. 2017 (abstract only).

Project timescale check: `data/timescale.csv` matches ICS chart v2024/12 exactly (Emsian base 410.62, Pragian base 413.02, Lochkovian base 419.62, Pridoli 422.7, Ludfordian 425.0, Gorstian 426.7, Dapingian 471.3, Darriwilian 469.4). All stage-to-Ma conversions in the four cards agree with it.

### Systematic patterns
1. No outright WRONG numbers. Every numeric value I could test (ages, sizes, counts, ranges) matches the source abstract or full text, including the fiddly ones (2,620 tetrads, 4 of 6 and 5 of 7 constrictions, 2,090 emergences, 41-58 nm lamellae). Errors are in attribution and hedging, not transcription.
2. Hedges lost in paraphrase (5 cases): Garza MDAs stated without bandwidth/Pb-loss sensitivity; Banks & Colthart "wounds that suggest... perhaps arthropods"; Edwards 2014 "some... others"; Earp 2024 "only partially answered"; Gerrienne 2006's "thalloid(?)" and three readings.
3. Absolute statements that a card's own references contradict (2 cases): "Silurian specimens preserve no anatomy" (vs Libertin 2022), and "Silurian record now rests on" two taxa (vs Earp's undecided localities and Hundt 1952).
4. Secondary or textbook sources behind modelling parameters: Psilophyton height_cm (Wikipedia, mis-cited) and axis_diameter_mm (three spot measurements) are the weakest quantitative fields across the four cards. Both are flagged in the card text but sit in the machine-readable traits block.
5. Timescale handling is sound: all Ma conversions follow ICS v2024/12 via `data/timescale.csv`, and old-timescale numbers (473-471, 415 Ma) are flagged. The one tension (407.4 Ma vs 410.62 Ma) arises because Doran & Tomescu treat 407.4 as "very near" the boundary, apparently on an older boundary value.
6. "Oldest/first" claims are well hedged. Where the card says "oldest generally accepted" (Cooksonia) it matches Kenrick 2012; C. barrandei is correctly presented as a contested older record. No overconfident "first" claims found.
7. Heavy reliance on one four-day-old paper (Hooper et al. 2026) for the cryptospore and tracheophyte timing narratives. The cards read it accurately, but the interpretations rest on a single unreplicated study.
8. Cards candidly mark unread sources (the Baragwanathia card especially), which made auditing easier and lowers risk. Items marked unverified in the cards were mostly confirmed true where I could check (Jaeger 1966, Tims & Chambers 1984, Kraft & Kvacek diagnosis).

### Fit as draft context
Fit for use as draft context. They are accurate on the facts that matter, honestly hedged, and the errors found are small and local. Before any number is used as a model input: (a) fix the two MISATTRIBUTED citations and the five hedge-loss items above; (b) treat Psilophyton `height_cm` and `axis_diameter_mm`, and the Garza MDAs, as not yet usable priors; (c) check the unverifiable Rubinstein & Vajda 2019 figures, the Strother 2015 criticism of the Argentine record, and the Cascales-Minana 2017 attributions against the papers. The Baragwanathia card is the strongest of the four; the cryptospore card is the most dependent on paywalled or brand-new sources.
