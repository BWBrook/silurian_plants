## 5. The four specific checks

(a) Claisse et al. 2025. Yes. Abstract: "Within the Lycophytina clade, zosterophylls form a grade paraphyletic to the lycopsids. A Sawdoniaceae clade of zosterophylls is sister to the lycopsids; the Sawdoniaceae-lycopsids clade is sister to a clade that includes the barinophytes." The full text adds that the Bayesian analysis recovered the same Sawdoniaceae clade and sister relationship ("strongest support to date") although overall topological congruence between parsimony and Bayesian is only 43.6% (17/39 nodes). Lycophytina comprises two clades; zosterophylls define the stem. The card states all of this. The only extra caveat worth adding: just one Zosterophyllum species (myretonianum) was sampled, so the polyphyly of the genus is not tested directly.

(b) Franks 2006. The numbers are exactly right: A = 2.47 +/- 0.29 umol m-2 s-1 (wheat 23.5 +/- 0.24); k_leaf = 1.30 +/- 0.21 mmol m-2 s-1 MPa-1 (wheat 15.4); drawdown 0.22 +/- 0.01 MPa, the smallest of 10 species. Whole branching photosynthetic stems were treated as single leaves on projected area; glasshouse, well watered, saturating light. DeltaPsi_leaf is an estimate. Reduced fern related to Ophioglossaceae: supported (Samec 2019: reduction from a more typical fern; Nitta 2022: Psilotales + Ophioglossales = Ophioglossidae, with horsetail position contested against nuclear data). Brownsey & Lovis's rhyniophyte reading is correctly dated and described as superseded.

(c) Ulothrix life cycle. State of evidence: (i) Classical haplontic account (zygote is the only diploid cell and undergoes meiosis) is the textbook inference for freshwater U. zonata; I found no primary cytological demonstration for the genus, and AlgaeBase (second hand) says convincing cytological or genetic confirmation is lacking. (ii) For marine Ulothrix (U. flacca var. roscoffensis) the settled zygote develops into a unicellular stalked Codiolum stage, called a sporophyte by Berger-Perrot et al. 1993. (iii) Horinouchi et al. 2019 show, in the ulotrichalean Monostroma angicava (not Ulothrix), that the Codiolum cell has a single endoreduplicated polyploid nucleus, grows very large, makes many zoospores and shows a meiosis-specific nuclear structure at zoosporogenesis, so it behaves as a sporophyte (heteromorphic haplo-diplontic cycle); they state the zygote-versus-sporophyte question is controversial and suggest the system is widespread in Ulotrichales. No study found applies those tests to Ulothrix itself. (iv) Leliaert et al. 2012 note molecular data give poor support for separating Ulotrichales from Ulvales on this life-history basis. Fairness of the card: good. It reports all four points accurately, attributes the Horinouchi result to Monostroma, admits it read no primary demonstration of zygotic meiosis in U. zonata, and labels its own inferences. Suggested tweaks: (1) the "In brief" sentence that the marine zygote "becomes a unicellular stalked sporophyte" should read "is described by Berger-Perrot et al. as"; (2) add that the disagreement is about whether the diploid cell is a growing sporophyte with mitosis (endoreduplication) before meiosis, not about where meiosis falls (my reading of the abstract); (3) correct the day-length wording (table row 6); (4) optionally cite the AlgaeBase life-history note once it can be read directly.

(d) Archaeopteris. Heights: the 11-32 m range is reported accurately (Table S1: 11.18-31.51 m, 18 trees, three estimators) and with the right main caveats ("approximations primarily designed to illustrate the approach"; root-based; conifer allometry; not attached to a trunk). One attribution error: the power law H = 3.21 DBH^0.6 is a generic conifer function from Hulshof et al. 2015, not calibrated on Araucaria; Araucaria was used only for the root-diameter to DBH step, from a small cultivated-tree pilot dataset. The DBH-route estimates span only 18.5-26.9 m; the extremes come from the direct trunk-base route. I read the author-accepted manuscript, not the published supplement; the card's numbers match it exactly. Age: ca. 385 Ma is Stein's own figure ("early mid Givetian (ca. 385 Ma)", by correlation of the Plattekill Fm with the marine Ludlowville Fm), 2-3 Myr older than Gilboa, timescale-dependent (Givetian 7.5 vs 5.0 Myr in the cited scales); the card notes correlation dating and assignment by comparison.
