# Drafted data requests (not sent; Barry decides)

Each request names the material and the exact missing fields. No reply is a dependency of any result.

## M1: productive material older than 515.5 Ma

**To:** the Zhushadong (Shandong) assemblage authors (Wang, Xu and Yin, Nanjing Institute of Geology and
Palaeontology); and, separately, the Tennessee Rome Formation authors (Strother; Taylor).

> We are assessing whether searched palynological material older than 515.5 Ma can constrain the
> absence premise of an embryophyte clock maximum. We record spore-like morphology separately from
> producer affinity. Could you share the complete sampling and processing roster for the sections
> containing your reported assemblages, including material below the lowest spore-bearing level and
> the positive material that could provide a reference? For each sample we need:
> - its identifier, stratigraphic position, lithology and independent age evidence;
> - whether it was collected, processed and searched, including failures and unsearched material;
> - preparation protocol, amount processed, slides or residues examined and search effort;
> - non-target palynomorph recovery and whether spore-like forms were sought in that same material;
> - detected, explicitly not detected or unresolved spore-like forms, with their identifications;
> - links among rock sample, preparation, slide, selected specimen and published illustration.
>
> Please include the section boundaries and sample-selection criteria, rather than only samples
> yielding spores. Barren preparations and uncertain identifications are valuable entries. If no
> material is independently dated older than 515.5 Ma, that is also useful to establish.

**Source-specific joins to request.** For Wang et al. (2022), link all thirteen declared shale
samples to the SI specimen catalogue and identify the material behind the 256-specimen composition
summary. The catalogue prefixes alone do not establish the complete processed-sample roster. For
Strother (2016) and Taylor & Strother (2024), link Rome sample SB01-14 and the Rome TEM specimens
(including R1O and R2A) to their source samples; keep younger Conasauga/Rogersville material separate.
These are identifiers to reconcile, not assumed matches between the studies.

**Fields needed:** section and sample ID; roster boundaries and selection rule; height/depth;
lithology; source age constraint and uncertainty; collected/processed/searched status; preparation
and search protocol; processed mass and examined effort; productivity (yes/no/unknown); non-target
recovery; spore-like search scope and outcome; morphology/identification uncertainty; preparation,
slide, specimen and figure links. Positive reference candidates need the same fields. An affinity
decision remains separate from search ascertainment.

## M3: searched-bed ledgers for the oldest polysporangiate bodies

**To:** the Loděnice (Barrandian) *Cooksonia barrandei* authors (Libertín and colleagues, National
Museum, Prague); and the Irish Cooksonia locality authors (Edwards and colleagues).

> We are assessing whether the searched rock record can test the absence premise of a 431.2 Ma
> crown-tracheophyte maximum. We score branched bodies with multiple sporangia separately from their
> inferred crown or stem placement. Do field or collection ledgers enumerate all eligible beds or
> blocks in the sampled section, including searched units with no such fossils, positive units and
> material that was inaccessible or not searched? For each unit we need:
> - position, lithology and age evidence, with uncertainty;
> - collection or search date, method and area, volume or time examined;
> - preservation quality and whether plant axes of any kind were recognised in that search;
> - presence, explicit non-detection or unresolved identification of bodies with multiple sporangia;
> - links from beds and blocks to collecting visits, slabs, fragments and museum specimens.
>
> Please include the selection boundaries and any younger material examined with comparable methods
> that could provide a reference. A complete roster is more useful than a list of successful finds
> or failed searches alone.

**Source-specific joins to request.** For Loděnice, distinguish the historical museum material from
new layer-4 collections and any searches of the other numbered lithological units. For Ireland,
distinguish the plant-bearing siltstone from interlaminated mudstone and record search methods in
each. A historical specimen count or geological thickness is not a count of searched blocks.

**Fields needed:** section/bed/block ID; roster boundaries and selection rule; position; independent
age constraint; lithology; accessible/searched status; collecting visit; search method and effort;
preservation class; plant-axis recovery and target-search scope in the same material; polysporangiate
outcome and diagnostic uncertainty; slab/fragment/specimen links. Younger reference material needs
the same reporting. Lithology, preservation and protocol differences remain explicit rather than
assuming a common detection rate from a denominator alone.
