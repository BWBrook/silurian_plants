# Drafted data requests (not sent; Barry decides)

Each request names the material and the exact missing fields. No reply is a dependency of any result.

## M1: productive material older than 515.5 Ma

**To:** the Zhushadong (Shandong) assemblage authors (Wang, Xu and Yin, Nanjing Institute of Geology and
Palaeontology); and, separately, the Tennessee Rome Formation authors (Strother; Taylor).

> We are testing whether the absence of cryptospore-like palynomorphs below Cambrian Stage 4 can
> constrain molecular-clock maxima for land plants (c. 515.5 Ma). Your published assemblages give the
> oldest occurrences. Could you share, for every sample processed from the sections below your
> lowest spore-bearing level:
> - stratigraphic position;
> - age evidence;
> - whether the sample was productive for any organic-walled microfossils;
> - whether spore-like forms were specifically searched for.
>
> Barren and unproductive samples matter as much as positive ones.

**Fields needed:** sample ID, height/depth, lithology, age constraint, productive (yes/no/unknown),
non-target palynomorphs recovered, cryptospore-like search performed, outcome.

## M3: searched-bed ledgers for the oldest polysporangiate bodies

**To:** the Loděnice (Barrandian) *Cooksonia barrandei* authors (Libertín and colleagues, National
Museum, Prague); and the Irish Cooksonia locality authors (Edwards and colleagues).

> For a sampling-model test of the 431.2 Ma maximum, we need the beds or blocks that were searched
> and yielded no polysporangiate fossils, alongside those that did. Do field or collection ledgers
> record, for each searched bed or block:
> - lithology;
> - extent or effort searched;
> - preservation quality;
> - whether plant axes of any kind were recovered?

**Fields needed:** bed/block ID, position, lithology, searched area or volume or time, preservation
class, plant axes present, polysporangiate bodies present, diagnostic uncertainty.
