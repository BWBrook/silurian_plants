# Charter

Status: **Stage 0, orientation** (opened 2026-10-26). No research question is chosen yet.

## The idea

Barry's starting point, in his words (chat, 2026-10-26): "a simulation modelling project on the
evolution of early land plants (late Ordovician to early Devonian), but it's a very nascent
thought". He frames it as "a long-term development project that might prove infeasible or
uninteresting, but is worth … at least exploring", with no hurry, and with the structure left
free to evolve.

## Why this interval

Between roughly 470 and 393 Ma, land plants went from a dispersed-spore record with no
recognisable bodies, through centimetre-scale leafless axes, to lycopsids and euphyllophytes with
leaves, roots, secondary-growth precursors and metre-scale height. Nearly every innovation that
defines vascular plants appears in that window: desiccation-tolerant spores, cuticle and
stomata, lignified conducting cells, branching sporophytes independent of the gametophyte,
apical dominance, leaves and roots. The same plants are implicated in changes to weathering,
atmospheric CO2 and O2, river form and soil. The record is unusually uneven, with abundant spores
and a few exceptional deposits (the Rhynie Chert above all) set against long gaps in
megafossils, so most explanations of the pattern remain verbal. That combination of a major
evolutionary transition, rich constraints and few quantitative tests is the case for modelling.

## What would make it worth doing

A framing earns a modelling programme if it meets all four tests:

1. **Discriminating evidence.** Competing hypotheses predict outcomes that the fossil, proxy or
   experimental record can tell apart: trait sequences, timing, size distributions, diversity
   or disparity patterns, geochemical signals.
2. **Novelty.** The model adds something the precedents (`docs/precedents.md`) do not: a
   mechanism, a coupling, a confrontation with data, or a reanalysis that changes a conclusion.
3. **Tractability.** The data can be assembled and the model run with resources on the mesh,
   without years of bespoke data collection.
4. **Interest.** Barry finds the question worth his attention.

If no framing passes tests 1 and 2 after orientation, the project is parked with a short written
account of why. That account is itself a useful output.

## Route

0. **Orientation** (current): taxon cards (`taxa/`), a period primer (`docs/primer.md`), a review
   of modelling precedents (`docs/precedents.md`) and candidate framings (`docs/framings.md`).
   Ends with a choice of framing, which is Barry's call on a recommendation from the agents.
1. **Sketch:** the smallest end-to-end model of the chosen framing, deterministic where possible.
2. **Ground:** null models, baselines and the data the model will confront (trait matrices built
   from the cards, occurrence data, proxy series).
3. **Enrich:** one mechanism at a time, each motivated by a discrepancy with data.
4. **Compare:** contrasts that discriminate between competing explanations.
5. **Synthesise:** what is robust, what depends on representation, and what is next.

Stages are a guide, not approval gates. Barry's operating principle applies: reversible work
proceeds and is shown afterwards; first public release, submission and outward-facing messages
are his decisions.

# Dispersed spores and other palynomorphs: Palynodata window extract

Extracted 2026-10-27 by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) on mac-mini for the project's dispersed-spore
gap (framing F3's spore channel, F4). This is an as-is extract: no ages converted, no names
harmonised, no duplicates merged. Building a compilation from it is project work.

## What Palynodata is

An index of palynomorph taxa reported in 22,152 documents of the palynological literature, built
from 1974 by an industry and academic consortium and released by Canada in 2008. The last
literature entered dates from 2006. Each `TAXON` row is one taxon in one document, with a region
as text, the oldest and youngest age names assigned, and numeric start and end ages from
Palynodata's own age table. It has no coordinates.

**Window rule.** A row is in the window if its numeric range (`agestart`, `ageend`) overlaps
383–485 Ma (76,336 rows), or if it has no numeric age and its age names match a window term
(15 rows; `derived_window_basis` says which). Rows with broad ages such as "Paleozoic" are included;
`derived_span_myr` and `derived_fully_inside_window` let them be filtered.

**Counts in the window.** By `TMIC`: acritarchs 41,483; chitinozoans 17,018; spores or pollen
14,114; scolecodonts 2,581; algae 361; dinoflagellates 325; other 194; fungal spores 113; unknown
affinity 88; plants 69; others 5. Of the 2,511 documents, 985 report spores and 1,526 report only
other palynomorphs. Ordovician-labelled spore rows: 738 from 135 documents, mostly Ashgill and
Caradoc.

## Read before use

- **Rows are mentions, not samples.** One assemblage can recur across papers; a taxon can appear
  in a synonymy list (`TSYN` set: 1,241 window spore rows) or be described as new (`TNEW` = X: 663)
  rather than reported from a fresh sample. Reworked or caved identifications are flagged in
  `TIDR` (15 window spore rows reworked).
- **Spores absent is not proven absent.** A document that lists only acritarchs or chitinozoans is
  evidence of sampling effort, but marine studies may not have counted or reported land-derived
  spores, and Palynodata indexes what a document names.
- **Ages are Palynodata's.** Age names predate current ICS usage (Gedinnian, Siegenian, Ludlovian,
  Gothlandian, Downtonian), and numeric ages come from its own 2006-era table, not from
  `../../timescale.csv`. Nothing has been converted.
- **Locations are text**, at region or country level ("Canada(NWT,Franklin)"); `TLOC` and the
  document's `GlobalLocation` are the only geography.
- **Coverage stops in 2006.** Later key records (for example Rubinstein et al. 2010, Middle
  Ordovician) are absent. The Global Acritarch Database (Shu et al. 2025, ESSD 17:3567) extended
  Palynodata's acritarchs with 2008–2023 literature and ICS v2024/12 ages; its data and code are in
  `../deposits/` as a method template.
- `TAGE` and `GenAge` are empty in the source; `TOLD`, `THYP`, `TYOU` and `TSUB` have no documented
  purpose.

## Reproduce

From `data/external/`: `python3 scripts/spores_palynodata.py`. It checks both checksums, unpacks
`paly.mdb` with `bsdtar`, exports tables with `mdb-export` (Homebrew `mdbtools`) and fetches the
Wellman et al. supplement from Figshare. Working files go to `local/spores/work/`.
