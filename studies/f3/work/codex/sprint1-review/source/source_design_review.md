## Why the Libya blank rule has source support

The evidence is stronger than a blank in a species chart alone:

Samples were deliberately selected for suitable lithologies and important intervals, so the inference remains conditional on this published, selected study population. Printed pp.33–35/PDF49–51 specify 20 g samples, 10 micrometre screening, variable oxidation and slide preparation. Printed p.35/PDF51 states that palynomorph groups included acritarchs, chitinozoans and cryptospores/spores, all specimens on the first palynological slide were counted and another slide scanned; other slides were scanned for rare forms. The separate 200-particle palynofacies count is not an E1 search count.
- **Event-specific recording:** the green header is “Cryptospores/Spores”. I freshly rendered and examined the H2 and I3 chart headers and plotted target columns (printed pp.622/624; PDF638/640). Systematics printed pp.327–329/PDF343–345 contain *A. dilutus* as the only taxon in the trilete-spore subsection. Printed p.328 distinguishes its defined trilete laesura from *Imperfectotriletes*' simple splitting. - **Independent productivity:** admission uses at least one marine-group symbol, not a trilete, cryptospore or total-spore mark. A target blank is therefore evaluated only where independent non-target recovery exists and the record is not excluded for an explicit barren/caved scope.
- **Age-group corroboration:** printed pp.380–381/PDF396–397 describe Cr2 as lacking true triletes. The explicit occurrence list does not cover every later formation-level assignment, so it should not be copied as an individual negative onto all older samples. Cr1's list is not an explicit negative. For the ten MZ Hawaz rows, the accepted zeros therefore depend on the declared complete-category chart/effort interpretation, not an invented Cr1 absence sentence.

This is a reasonable mapping to **reported encounters**, with a source-specific completeness assumption. It does not certify perfect reporting. The sole explicit core-positive at E1 7287 ft is absent from its chart (printed pp.381–382/PDF397–398); the adapter preserves this as a separate core alternative.
