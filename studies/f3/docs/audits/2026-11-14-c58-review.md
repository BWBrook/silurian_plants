## Statistical sources

Hanley and Lippman-Hand (1983) was read in full from the author's
[McGill reprint](https://jhanley.biostat.mcgill.ca/Reprints/If_Nothing_Goes_1983.pdf),
pp. 1743–1745. Equation 1 on p. 1744 gives the exact zero-count binomial boundary;
the following expansion gives the rule-of-three approximation. The fossil-specific
conversion to expected early finds at the estimated later rate follows from the
odds transformation, rather than being a result asserted from that source.
Both authors and the full pagination match the reprint and
[PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6827763/); Crossref omits Lippman-Hand and
gives only the first page. The manual bibliography entry is correct.

Both DOIs were checked independently against Crossref. The publisher abstract of
[Jovanovic and Levy (1997)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00031305.1997.10473947)
supports the generic binomial rule cited in Methods. Its full text was not read;
the gap is recorded in `docs/closed-literature.csv`. C58's independent derivation
and the fully read original source do not depend on obtaining it. Source provenance
and the copyrighted reprint hash are recorded in `library/closed.tsv`; the PDF is
git-ignored and excluded from the public repository.
