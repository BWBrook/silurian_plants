## What this frame can test

The current main occurrence reading (`S-w22-S2-hooper`) accepts Sandbian E2
but no secure E1; the two pre-Sandbian E2 reports remain disputed by Wellman
et al. (2022), table 1 footnote *. Applying those readings consistently leaves
**no positive E1 reference observation in this core under main**. A complete
inventory does not by itself supply a supported positive encounter rate.
An inclusive E1 analysis is a different declared reading, with seven positive
depths, and only three samples stratigraphically below its first report at
48.2 m. Which of those three is older than 459.1 Ma depends on the age model.

The aggregate main reading also deserves finer adjudication: Hooper et al.'s
published compression concern explicitly names the **Dalby** material, whereas
the 34 and 33.05 m reports are in the **Skagen** part of the succession. This
tranche preserves the existing reading and flags its scope; it does not invent
a specimen-specific rejection or quietly promote those two reports to secure
E1. A choice of reference frame, eligibility classes and any pooling must be
declared under each reading before fitting.

Hooper et al.'s Supplementary Table 4, C_4 (PDF pp.16–18), explicitly identifies
the probable 48.2 m specimen as the fossil behind the 459.1 Ma soft maximum.
That number is the historical Sandbian base, 458.4 Ma, plus its quoted 0.7 Myr
uncertainty. It is not a measurement of the spore or a bound estimated from
the Borenshult absences. Our ICS table instead gives a 458.2 Ma Sandbian base.
Retain the published calibration being tested and its provenance; do not
replace its number silently with a recalculated one.

An ancestral trait state can imply that a trait originated by a node. It does
not establish that the oldest **observed fossil** predates that node. The local
absence test addresses encounter intensity conditional on its declared frame,
readings and biological assumptions. Translating it into a crown-age result
also requires trait-to-node placement, persistence and geographic/detection
assumptions. Reusing the calibration's own fossil is a direct audit of that
evidence, not independent corroboration of the calibration.

## Age and dependence evidence for Claude's next design

The mapped stage membership is three late Darriwilian samples, 30 Sandbian
and one early Katian. The source numerical contexts are preserved separately
from current ICS stage boundaries. Neither a within-stage density nor an
age-depth relation is estimated here.

- The 2019 figure 3 places 43.1, 41.9 and 41.1 m in *B. alobatus*; the 2023
  figure 3 places them in *B. gerdae*. The 2023 assemblage prose puts the
  transition between 41.9 and 41.1 m. These are three source readings of a
  shared boundary, not independent per-sample alternatives to average.
- The 2023 figure 2 places 38.35/38.06 m in Dalby and 36.7 m in Skagen.
  The general prose gives Dalby's top at 38.35 m; pp.64–65 give a main
  bentonite interval of 38.06–36.7 m and another at 39.20–38.98 m. The
  lithological alternatives are retained. Published thicknesses of 1.8 m
  (2019) and approximately 1.55 m here (2023) do not establish exact contacts.
- The 454.41 ± 0.17 Ma age is cited for the equivalent ash on Bornholm.
  Its application is a shared correlation, not 34 local radiometric dates.
  The primary uncertainty convention has not been checked; no Normal SD is
  inferred from the printed ± term. The 2023 figure caption's Bell/Sell
  citation discrepancy is retained in the age-source discussion below.
- The 2019 context places 67.6 m at approximately 460 Ma and the deeper
  71.1 m near the basal approximately 461 Ma label. They are candidates
  older than 459.1 Ma under that calibration, not exactly measured ages.
  The approximately 459 Ma assignment at 59.1 m cannot force its side of
  the threshold. Stage names alone provide no quantified threshold crossing.

Proposed diagnostics, **not adopted distributions**: ordered ages for one
34-sample core; shared stage and subzone boundaries; a common ash age and
contact interpretation; source-wide alternative correlation scenarios; and
facies/count sensitivity for the low-yield bentonite material. The
`shared_age_group` label identifies dependence and does not assert that all
34 samples are coeval. Stage-only current-ICS and published-calibration
scenarios should remain distinct. Actual-design qualification must include
the chosen first-appearance rule and any reused discovery/reference sample.

Sources already held supply this extraction. A bounded manifest check found
