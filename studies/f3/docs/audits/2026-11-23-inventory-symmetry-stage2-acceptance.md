## Which count and age can occur together

That attainable count and age give a **428.84 Ma steady-rate limit on the start of the recognisable crown vascular-plant record** (C27). In regional rows, S3-A25 groups localities by their midpoint stages first and then varies inclusion at the cutoff. These limits are conditional on the fixed groups. ## Verification and timing

At a fixed count, an older first find moves the limit older under each of the six positive rate histories. At a fixed oldest age, adding finds moves the limit younger. Once a cutoff-straddling unit supplies the oldest age, adding another unit cannot give an older limit at that age. This explains why S3-A25 can use a short list of count–age pairs to recover the exhaustive result. The test for the smallest early recording contrast optimises the ratio separately.

The production run in a separate output directory matched all five committed files byte for byte. An independent reconstruction from the signed units, Capel rows, stage boundaries and collection-effort table checked all 1,918 Task 4 rows in 43 inventory/run combinations. Across 274 settings it tried all 1,002 inclusion patterns for units straddling a cutoff. The finite history limits agreed within 0.000000005 Ma. All 1,330 earlier Task 4 rows and 2,175 earlier test rows retain their original fields. Excluding Yea from the literal Capel reading reproduces all 28 test rows and 42 Task 4 rows for the corresponding main reading. The pinned manuscript check passed with all 62 current claims cited.
