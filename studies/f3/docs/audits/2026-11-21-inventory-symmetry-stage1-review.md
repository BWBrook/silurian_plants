## What the crosswalk and the tests can establish

- **Match at the resolution of the source.** Karlštejn–Kosov is one published *Baragwanathia* holotype (KR 1) with conflicting locality labels. Retain both labels and one occurrence. Bathurst is a four-site aggregation. Hoboksar row 1130 matches the formation and district, without identifying Mangkelu II. Trubchin row 1191 matches village and member, without establishing the bed. Skala has no separately identifiable equivalent. Under the submitted mapping both sources have six named Silurian regions, but only **four** midpoint region–stage cells coincide. Laurentia and Gondwana–South America fall in the Přídolí in S0 and the Ludfordian in S3. Neither the shared regions nor the eight versus 13 named Silurian units establishes the direction of bias in the fraction. [Karlštejn specimen](../../../../taxa/baragwanathia-brevifolioides.md) · [Submitted crosswalk](2026-11-21-inventory-symmetry.md)
- **Use identifiable regional units.** Germany, Russia and Poland intentionally map to `multiple` in the inherited code. Capel rows 47, 158 and 556 then occupy one artificial Emsian cell. Keep the historical pooled calculation for comparison. Predeclare the regional comparison on named palaeoregions across all inventories, omit unresolved codes from geographic breadth, and report when pooling changes a decision. Use the same identified cells for the numerator and denominator. The legacy numerator counts distinct (region, minimum age, maximum age) tuples, while the denominator counts region–midpoint-stage cells. Reproduce that pair separately. [Regional code definition](../../data/occurrences/README.md) · [Legacy calculation](../../models/loop3/switch_reading.py)
- **Respect the direction of each tail.** For a fixed inventory size, test an excess at the smallest attainable interval count and a deficit at the largest. Each choice gives the largest one-sided p-value over the allowed ages. The current harness uses the lower tail at the minimum and leaves the regional maximum blank. Even in frozen S0, 13 of 48 must be late Silurian and 16 could be, owing to the three Yea ranges. A lower bound on the share can still use the guaranteed count. A two-sided rejection across ages requires rejection at every attainable count. Condition explicitly on the guaranteed whole-window inventory or assess attainable pairs of total and interval counts jointly if cutoff membership varies. Implement the per-proxy smallest upper-tail rejecting count and the level-model largest lower-tail rejecting count requested for S2. Report the preliminary deficit calculation only with its age assignment until the test is rerun. [Stage 1 harness](../../models/loop3/inventory_symmetry_tests.py)
- **Limit the S3 verdict to its observations.** Endpoint listings omit intervening finds. Regional occupancy reduces the influence of multiple species endpoints in one cell, but cells containing only range-through taxa can still disappear. S3 locality counts and endpoint arithmetic can be descriptive. The S3 regional result can enter a decision as a comparison of *listed endpoint cells*. A binomial tail for those cells has no demonstrated calibration for a fossil-finding rate. I agree with omitting the S3 age-shape test. For gate 4, mark the S3 evidence unavailable and judge the conditional S0/S1/S2 results on their own stated inventories. The requirement for agreement with S3 cannot be fulfilled for this test. Show which S3 reading and counting convention each other gate used. [Amendment A9](2026-11-21-inventory-symmetry-s3-rules.md) · [Original decision rules](2026-11-21-inventory-symmetry-spec.md)
