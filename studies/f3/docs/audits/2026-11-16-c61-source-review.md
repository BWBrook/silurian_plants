# C61 source and inventory review

Codex, 16 November 2026. Reviewed `ed538ba`, concentrating on Results (i), Table 2 and supplement S5.11. This is a source check, not a rebuilt inventory or independent mathematical check. No source table, code, claim or manuscript was changed in this review.

## Findings requiring correction or clarification

1. **The reported age of *Capesporangites* is already available.** Direct access to the ScienceDirect page returned HTTP 403, but its publicly indexed publisher text contains the Conclusions and locality sections. The Conclusions assign the material to the upper Silurian, Přídolí. The generic diagnosis describes branching leafless axes and terminal sporangia; the locality section gives Kosov quarry and a loose block. Consequently, a detailed horizon remains unread, but the published stage is known and younger than *C. barrandei*. The manuscript and `docs/closed-literature.csv` should ask for bed/provenance details, rather than leave open whether the published material is older than *C. barrandei*.

   Primary source: [Uhlířová, Pšenička and Sakala (2024), publisher page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0034666723002178), DOI `10.1016/j.revpalbo.2023.105048`. Crossref was independently queried on 16 November and confirms the three authors, title, March 2024, volume 322 and article 105048. The full paper was not read.

2. **A missing taxon need not add a sampling unit.** The reviewed inventory already contains `tail:M-bohemica`, main B1, at `Czech Republic|kosov quarry, prague basin`, Přídolí (422.7–419.62 Ma). *Capesporangites* is absent as a taxon, but an additional locality–age unit is not demonstrated by the readable information. A finer bed or locality distinction would require source adjudication. Do not state that a younger horizon necessarily adds one find.

3. **One to four additions is a sensitivity, not a completed census.** The sources support B1 membership at Freshwater East, Targrove and Brown Clee Hill. The Wellman conference abstract establishes eophyte bodies at Ludford Lane, but does not show that the Ludford material itself preserves branched fertile axes. Its B1 admission remains to be checked. Moreover, the E3 screening restriction to Homerian and older records leaves other younger omissions unassessed. The four-addition calculation can illustrate endpoint sensitivity to the named leads; it cannot bound the size of all inventory omissions or certify that exactly four independent B1 units are missing.

4. **Keep conducting tissue separate from crown membership.** Morris et al. (2024) establish tracheid evidence in *T. offaeus*, while retaining uncertainty about its position within total-group tracheophytes. This does not establish crown membership, and absence of such evidence in *T. transwalliensis* is not the sole reason its crown placement remains uncertain. The present supplement's final causal clause should preserve that distinction if revised.

## Checks that pass

- **Eophytes.** The held `library/closed/edwards2021-open_wiley.pdf` is *Piecing together the eophytes*, DOI `10.1111/nph.17703`. Materials and methods locate the fossils at north Brown Clee Hill and give an early Lochkovian age; printed Table 2 (p. 1451) identifies published eophytes from that locality and includes branching *Fusiformitheca* and *Ficoiditheca*. The diagnosis and anatomical discussion support small plants, predominantly dichotomising axes, terminal capsules, cryptospores and lack of water-conducting cells. Some included forms are unbranched or insufficiently known, so individual B1 admission still requires actual branching evidence.
- **Ludford Lane.** The held one-page `library/closed/wellman2026_macbook20261030.pdf` explicitly names late Silurian (Pridoli) Ludford Lane and Early Devonian (Lochkovian) North Brown Clee Hill. Its conference-abstract status is accurately stated. It supplies no specimen-level branching account for Ludford Lane.
- **Freshwater East.** I rendered and read the relevant pages of the image-only held `library/closed/edwards1979_firststudy.pdf`: printed p. 26 discusses branched fertile forms with terminal sporangia, and p. 32 describes a Freshwater *C. hemisphaerica* axis with a sporangium on each fork. These support B1 inclusion independently of any claimed lycophyte placement. Printed p. 42 declines to infer that the whole *Tortilicaulis* plant was unbranched or nonvascular from its incomplete fragments. The manuscript's fragment wording is accurate.
- **Targrove and species identity.** The held `library/closed/morris2024_firststudy.pdf`, printed p. 137, reports branching and in situ trilete monads in *T. transwalliensis* from lower Lochkovian Targrove. Materials and methods (p. 138) identify the new anatomical specimens as *T. offaeus* from Brown Clee Hill, early to middle Lochkovian. The Discussion (p. 141) presents the tracheid interpretation and leaves stem/crown placement unresolved. Thus the species and locality correction in S5.11 is supported.
- **Inventory representation.** A targeted read of all reviewed-horizon rows matching Freshwater, Targrove, Brown Clee, Ludford, Capesporangites and Kosov finds only the existing Kosov main/inclusive/outlier rows and Freshwater East inclusive/outlier rows. This independently confirms the named omissions and Freshwater East's absence from the main reading; it does not establish inventory completeness under alternative spellings or uninspected source records.

## Scope and disposition

The nested categories are useful for separating any recognisable land-plant body, branched fertile sporophytes and diagnosed crown vascular-plant membership. Source evidence for branching is not evidence of crown placement. The parent review owns the separate question of whether Hooper et al.'s calibration rationale is accurately described and whether a body-record endpoint can be read as a clock-calibration maximum.

The named younger records do not move the oldest admitted age on their reported stages. They do expose incomplete younger screening, which should remain explicit when interpreting counts and shape tests. The additional-unit bounds have not been checked in this source review.
