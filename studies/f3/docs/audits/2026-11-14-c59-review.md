## Decision

**C59 is supported with the inferential qualifications below.** The combined
measure permits the tested older ancestors in the early-absence calculation,
but predicts almost no late-Silurian finds when transferred proportionally
through time. Using only the difference between its window averages remains a
separate assumption. The Abstract's combined-measure sentence and the Conclusion
retain their plain wording and that distinction.

## What is verified

The independent integral route gives rock × spore ratios 21.668070 and
29.757330 at 444 and 452 Ma, versus stored 21.6704 and 29.7603. At the fitted
48-find later intensity these correspond to about 1.15 and 1.23 expected early
finds, below the C58 boundary of 3.09. The pointwise product's expectation is
non-decreasing with candidate age, so its fit at 452 Ma also covers the
intermediate ages down to 444 Ma. Searching × spore share exceeds the boundary
at 48 but falls below it at 24 and fewer tested finds.

For Gorstian–Pridoli, exact integration predicts 0.14107785 finds under the
rock × spore product for 48 independent finds, versus 13 dated wholly inside
that interval. The same 13 also lie wholly in Ludfordian–Pridoli. Valid age
supports permit 13–16 locality-age finds and six or seven regional units;
non-rejection of level finding or searching at the guaranteed minimum is not
validation of either history. The North American proxy is being transferred
to a global inventory; its zeros are not evidence of globally absent rock.

Detailed routes, tolerances and interpretation are in
`2026-11-14-c59-integrals-review.md` and
`2026-11-14-c59-placement-review.md`. The parent inspected the inputs, unit
membership, formulas and final wording, reproduced the production tables to a
temporary directory, then integrated both checks. Both independent reviewers
accepted the resulting Methods, Results, Conditions and estimands repairs.
