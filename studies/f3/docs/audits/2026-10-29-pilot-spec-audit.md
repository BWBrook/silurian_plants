**What the audit changed.** The draft's headline structural claim, that M1 and M2 rest on
opposite readings of the ancestral spore, did not survive. The corrected point is narrower and
still useful:
- the two crown maxima need different temporal propositions;
- the traditional reading removes M2's bearing on the crown;
- under Hooper et al.'s reading, M1's bearing depends on when cryptospore production arose
  relative to the crown, which no published tree dates.
