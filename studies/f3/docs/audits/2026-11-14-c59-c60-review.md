## Interpretation retained

The proposed innovation-triggered expansion remains in a paragraph explicitly
labelled interpretation. Population multiplication at a chosen rate illustrates
a possibility, while slow net growth, dispersal, habitat availability, evolution,
preservation and searching remain possible controls on finding. Only under the
biological-spread interpretation would a switch date spread; preservation or
searching can produce a similar record pattern.

The source check supports the three cautions, but not a universal Cooksonia spore
assignment, a demonstrated transport route for every fragment or a body identity
for the oldest dispersed triletes. The earlier structural summary's exhaustive
switch-or-delay choice is narrowed in the current decision log and questions
register. No programme question changes status.
