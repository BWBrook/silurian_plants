## Recommendation

Keep the adopted *Proc B* target and the proposed allocation of four figures and up to two tables.
Build the narrative around three analytical questions and their answers. The one-page draft needs

## Message and structure

**The present headline overstates the result.** “Supports a young origin ... only if ... as often”
turns a conditional fossil-record bound into support for a clock, makes equal recording a necessary
condition, and applies one recording condition to three different evidential problems. The M1 account
is an unquantified preservation-control argument; Murzuq constrains local delivered spore encounter;
the B2 frontier quantifies requirements for a recorded body category and an assumed mapping to a node.
Even the B2 bound changes with unit definition while holding constant recording. The headline's
necessary-condition claim therefore fails independently of the prose style.

One positive replacement, **22 words**:

> The fossil record allows older plant origins when early remains were found and identified less
> often, or distinctive body features appeared later.

This expresses compatibility under stated alternatives without selecting a clock or calling the
recording contrast measured. In the paper, “found and identified less often” must refer to the rate of
recorded identifiable occurrences, which also reflects abundance and geographic range. The separate
results still need their separate scope. Preserve Claude's version as the version on which Barry
commented, and mark any replacement as Codex's proposal until reconciled.

**H1–H3 should become questions or analytical expectations, with their retrospective status stated.**

| Label | What it currently contains | Recommended treatment |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | A useful evidence-assessment rubric plus the observed finding that the maxima have different gaps | Ask what evidence and assumptions support each maximum. A weakest-link framework is not itself an independently tested biological prediction. |
| H2 | A general taphonomic rationale and the locality/delivery scope of an observation model | Ask what sustained older trilete encounter the Murzuq cuttings permit. The study has no matched spore-versus-body sampling experiment establishing that spore absence is generally more informative. |
| H3 | The known effect of sample size on a fixed-rate interval plus the observed match to one clock | Ask what recording and diagnostic-delay combinations permit candidate ages. A confidence bound close to the oldest fossil does not predict which external clock it will overlap. |

The body design says explicitly that it is not a preregistration before the pilot
(`studies/f3/docs/loop3-design.md`). The reconstructed H1–H3 headings should therefore carry no “a priori” or
“we expected” history without contemporaneous evidence for those specific predictions. A clear aim
and analysis-led paper does not require retrospective hypotheses to be presented as prospective.

For the one-page structure, retain Background → Questions → Approach → Results → Assumptions →
Conclusions. Match the three results to the questions. The first paragraph should name the three clock
studies and distinguish the common ancestor of living groups from first terrestrial plants. The
approach should define “recording” once, in ordinary words. The conditions section can gather detailed
assumptions and sensitivity values while the result nouns retain their scientific objects: local
encounter, category bound and compatibility requirement. Concentrating caveats cannot turn any of
those into an abundance reconstruction, a crown date or a verdict between clocks.

The final promise that measuring recording “would decide between” the clocks is too strong. That
measurement would constrain the rate–delay alternatives; geographic delivery and character-to-node
mapping would still matter. Recommend a positive conclusion about narrowing the combinations of
recording and diagnostic histories compatible with each age.

## Display plan: retain the allocation, specify the inferential objects

Four figures and two tables are a sensible editorial budget, not a verified journal maximum.
The existing “three more tables than this paper can carry” should mean three more than this chosen
budget, rather than an official restriction inferred from five comparators.

**Brief:** retain the clock/fossil timeline as the default while the reconstructed paper is being
settled. A complete frontier introduces a second intensity axis, multiple unit rules and diagnostic
delays, and needs more explanation than a newcomer receives from a small figure. A simplified
frontier or a two-panel timeline/requirements figure can be tested later in the actual two-page
layout. The claim that the full Fig. 4b would automatically serve the brief better is presently a
design proposal, not an established improvement.

## Venue and current rule verification

*Proc B* is an appropriate adopted target for this empirical assessment of an evolutionary timing
problem. The contribution is the source-specific synthesis and quantified conditional requirements;
the reconstruction should lead with that biological question rather than claim a new general
confidence-interval theory. The publisher lists *Proceedings B* under biological science; acceptance

unverified. No need to revisit the venue decision or solve that access issue to improve steps 1–2.
