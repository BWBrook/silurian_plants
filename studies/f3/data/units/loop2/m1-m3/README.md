## M1: the affinity of the Cambrian Stage 4 forms (decided)

`m1_affinity.csv` applies the adopted E2 addendum (`studies/f3/data/units/e2-unit-rule.md` v1) to the three held
sources describing Stage 4 cryptospore-like forms and related younger Cambrian material. On Axis 1
they are packets, dyads, tetrads and irregular polyads, with evidence on wall ultrastructure and
development. On Axis 2 none of these three sources explicitly accepts the assessed Stage 4 producers
as embryophytes:

- Strother 2016 places the taxa in Charophyta;
- Taylor & Strother 2024 interpret them as meiotic products of terrestrial charophytic algae,
  likely formed without a sporangium;
- Wang et al. 2022 judge them "unlikely directly related to embryophytes", while acknowledging
  that investigation remains premature.

**Verdict.** Under main they are not accepted as E2, which confirms the existing reading; this does
not turn them into negative trials. Morphology, ultrastructure and inferred development supply
evidence for precursor characters, but do not independently establish embryophyte producers. The
inclusive reading remains an explicitly declared alternative acceptance, requiring its record-specific
rationale. This review does not revoke the historical inclusive assignments. Taylor & Strother's
clock discussion motivates investigating older spores; it does not establish circularity in an
affinity assignment.

**Material and age scope.** Strother's Rome sample SB01-14 has a Stage 4 assignment in the text
(p.33), with a broader, qualified Series 2 assignment in Fig.1. The 514.5–506.5 Ma interval is the
project's ICS v2024/12 stage conversion, not a numerical date measured by Strother. Wang's source
interval is approximately 514–509 Ma; its modern stage conversion is retained separately. Taylor &
Strother's Rome TEM packets include R1O and R2A. The unnamed dyad/monad compared with *Riccia* on
p.103 is Rogersville material (Rog 2ox-1-1), so that comparison must not inherit Rome's Stage 4 age.

**Consequence for M1.** Every age within 514.5–506.5 Ma is younger than the 515.5 Ma maximum.
Accepting a Stage 4 E2 occurrence changes accepted occurrence support, but does not itself contradict
that maximum or establish crown membership. An absence test of M1 still needs productive, completely
reported material older than 515.5 Ma and an admissible reference/encounter design; no assessed frame
supplies one (Loop 1). Neither the inclusive affinity assumption nor a stage boundary supplies that
missing evidence.

**Related lead, recorded and closed.** Vecoli et al. (2017, p.320) note that small tetrads in the
Argentine Dapingian assemblage were "erroneously attributed to *Tetrahedraletes medinensis* in
Rubinstein et al., 2010". This corrects a taxonomic assignment while retaining identifiable tetrads
and monads; it does not establish embryophyte affinity or turn the sample into an absence. For
`RIOCAP-7999`, the current E2 outcome remains **unresolved under main, detected under inclusive and
outlier** (`studies/f3/data/units/outcomes.csv`). This particular correction changes none of those outcomes.
Separately, its Dapingian age supplies no direct pre-515.5 Ma exposure. The lead is closed without
claiming that either the sample's affinity dispute or its missing denominator has been resolved.
